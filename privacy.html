Privacy policy.

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Arthandler is a puzzle game about moving art into storage. Your progress belongs to you, and stays on your device in the current game implementation.

01

Data processed by the game

Arthandler does not require registration or an account. The currently inspected game code has no advertisements, third-party analytics, tracking scripts, or network requests. It does not transmit your gameplay data to the developer.

Progress information such as reached and completed jobs, saved positions, move counts, personal bests and the sound setting is saved in local device storage to enable continuing your game.

02

Why local data is used

Local game data is used solely for saving progress and preferences, and for restoring a job after the game is closed and reopened. No account is necessary.

03

Sharing and third parties

The currently inspected game implementation does not send gameplay information to the developer or other parties. When you access Arthandler through Google Play, Google's own services may process data according to Google's privacy terms. This policy does not cover data independently processed by Google Play, your operating system or your device manufacturer.

This promotional website is hosted through GitHub Pages. The promotional website does not contain advertising scripts, analytics or tracking cookies. It loads the Inter font from Google Fonts, which may receive technical request information (such as an IP address) when you visit the website. See Google's Privacy Policy. GitHub may process technical access information as the hosting provider. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

04

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until it is removed, for example by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Whether app data may be restored by device backups depends on your operating system and backup settings. The developer does not maintain a server-side copy of your saved game in the inspected implementation.

05

Children's privacy

Arthandler does not intentionally ask for personal information or create user profiles in the inspected game implementation. If future versions introduce new data practices, this policy will be updated.

06

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app, its Android packaging, or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
